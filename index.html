Documentary LC & Goods Declaration File (Synthetic)

Red Flags Inspected: 1 / 4
IMPORTER / APPLICANT Crescent Agro-Textiles Pvt. Ltd. (Karachi)
DECLARED LINE OF BUSINESS (CRP) Raw Cotton & Apparel Yarn Trading
FIELD 45A — DESCRIPTION OF GOODS & HS CODE High-Precision CNC Dual-Use Maraging Steel Rotors (HS 8458.11)
Flag #1
COMMERCIAL INVOICE VS. GD / MARKET BENCHMARK Declared Unit Price: USD 14,800/unit (Market Benchmark: USD 6,200/unit)
Flag #2
BILL OF LADING & MARITIME AIS SCREENING Vessel "MV NORTHERN STAR" — 96-Hr AIS Dark Activity near High-Risk Transshipment Hub
Flag #3
REMITTANCE & BENEFICIARY BANK LOCATION Goods Shipped from East Asia; Payment Routed to Unrelated Third-Party Shell Entity in Offshore Free Zone
Flag #4
RED FLAG #1 — CUSTOMER RISK PROFILE (CRP) & DUAL-USE MISMATCH

Line-of-Business Inconsistency & Dual-Use Proliferation Risk

Documentary Finding

The applicant (Crescent Agro-Textiles) is profiled under Textile Yarn & Raw Cotton trading, yet the LC and Goods Declaration cover High-Precision CNC Dual-Use Maraging Steel Rotors (HS 8458.11).

Recommended TBML Compliance Action

Trigger Trade Customer Risk Profiling (CRP) escalation; require Dual-Use / Strategic Export Control verification and end-user undertaking before LC processing.
